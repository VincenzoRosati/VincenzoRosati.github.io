### Abstract

This paper addresses the optimal placement of sensors in a cyclonic burner operating under MILD (Moderate or Intense Low-oxygen Dilution) combustion conditions. The study develops a methodology for sparse sensor placement to efficiently monitor key combustion parameters while minimizing hardware costs.

### Key Contributions

- Novel sparse sensor placement methodology
- Application to cyclonic burner systems
- MILD combustion monitoring strategies
- Cost-effective sensor network design

### Abstract

This work investigates the scale-down of cyclonic flow fields under MILD combustion operating conditions. The study provides insights into maintaining flow characteristics and combustion stability when reducing burner dimensions, crucial for practical applications and industrial scaling.

### Key Contributions

- Scale-down methodology for cyclonic burners
- MILD combustion flow field analysis
- Scaling laws and correlations
- Industrial application insights

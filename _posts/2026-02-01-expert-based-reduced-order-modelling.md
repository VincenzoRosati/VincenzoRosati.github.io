### Abstract

This paper presents an expert-based reduced-order modeling approach for simulating urban air flow and pollutant dispersion. The methodology incorporates wind-rose-informed clustering to efficiently capture the essential dynamics of urban atmospheric conditions while significantly reducing computational requirements.

### Key Contributions

- Expert-based reduced-order modeling framework
- Wind-rose-informed clustering methodology
- Urban air flow and pollutant dispersion simulation
- Computational efficiency improvements
- Practical urban air quality assessment

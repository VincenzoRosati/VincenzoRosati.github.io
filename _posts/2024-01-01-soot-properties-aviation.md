### Abstract

This study presents a comprehensive characterization of soot properties in an aviation Jet A-1 laminar non-premixed coflow flame. The research investigates the formation and characteristics of soot particles under controlled combustion conditions, providing insights into pollutant formation in aviation fuel combustion.

### Key Findings

- Detailed soot property analysis in Jet A-1 combustion
- Characterization of laminar non-premixed flame behavior
- Implications for aviation fuel combustion efficiency
